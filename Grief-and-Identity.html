Grief & Identity

Stop grieving the person you thought you'd be. Why letting go of an old timeline is a step forwars, not a failure.

Grief & Identity 3-4 min read

Why letting go is a progress

Releasing an old identity is not a collapse. It is a clearing.

When my fashion business closed, I thought I had ruined my future. I thought I had stepped off the path I was meant to be on. I thought I had failed.

With time, I realised something gentler. I was not collapsing. I was recalibrating.

Letting go of that identity made space for the person I was becoming. Someone who had lived through real endings. Someone who had learned resilience. Someone with wisdom my younger self could not have imagined.

Letting go is not a failure. It is choosing reality over nostalgia. It is choosing truth over expectation. It is profoundly courageous.

The non obvious insights

Most people think identity is built by adding things. Achievements. Milestones. Roles. But identity is often strengthened by subtraction.

You become more yourself when you stop dragging old timelines behind you. You become lighter when you stop trying to resurrect outdated dreams. You become clearer when you stop negotiating with ghosts.

Your life is not late. It is simply unfolding on a different schedule than the one you once imagined.

A gentle coaching invitation

If you feel stuck, it may not be because you lack discipline or motivation. It may be because you are still grieving a version of yourself you once lived. A version you were not ready to lose.

Perhaps today is the day you allow that grief to soften.

Not by force. Not by pretending it did not matter. But by acknowledging that the person you are now the one who has lived through real endings and real beginnings deserves to lead the story.

If this resonates, reach out to me and let's talk about where you're stuck.
