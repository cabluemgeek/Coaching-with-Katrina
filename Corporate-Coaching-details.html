The Work

Investing in people.

People are at the heart of every successful organisation. When employees are equipped with the tools to manage stress effectively, communicate confidently, and adapt to challenges, they are better able to perform, collaborate, and thrive.

In today's fast-paced workplace, increasing workloads, constant change, and competing priorities can impact wellbeing, productivity, engagement, and performance. Left unmanaged, these pressures can contribute to overwhelm, absenteeism, burnout, and staff turnover.

At Coaching with Katrina, we partner with organisations to help employees manage stress, strengthen resilience, improve communication, and develop sustainable work practices that support both wellbeing and performance.

Our programmes combine professional training, practical workplace strategies, guided reflection, breathwork, and mindfulness techniques to create meaningful and lasting change.

Why It Matters

Organisational Benefits

  • Improved employee wellbeing and morale
  • Increased productivity and focus
  • Greater resilience and adaptability
  • Enhanced communication and collaboration
  • Reduced workplace stress
  • Reduced stress-related absenteeism
  • Improved employee retention
  • Stronger workplace culture
  • Sustainable long-term performance

How It's Delivered

Programme Delivery

All programmes are delivered through engaging and interactive group training sessions designed to provide practical tools and immediate workplace application.

Sessions Include

  • Professional presentations and training
  • Interactive group discussions
  • Practical workplace exercises
  • Reflection activities
  • Stress management tools
  • Emotional regulation techniques
  • Breathwork and mindfulness practices
  • Action planning and implementation strategies

Delivery Format

  • One session every two weeks
  • 3 to 4 hours per session
  • Available onsite, online, or hybrid
  • Suitable for teams, departments, and organisation-wide initiatives
  • Recommended group size: 10 to 30 participants
  • Larger groups available upon request
