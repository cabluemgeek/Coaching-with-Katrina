02 — Corporate Coaching

Build healthier, more resilient teams.

A long-term wellbeing partnership for companies who want their people to thrive, not just survive.

The Work

Healthier teams, built to last.

Corporate Coaching is a long-term wellbeing partnership designed for companies who want healthier, more resilient teams. It's offered as structured packages — 3-month, 6-month, 12-month, and extended multi-year plans.

Every plan is shaped around your organisation's real pressures, so support feels practical, not theoretical.

1:1 Sessions

Focus areas include

  • Burnout prevention and cure — stabilising, regulating, and rebuilding capacity.
  • Emotional regulation — tools employees can use in real time.
  • Handling extreme workplace stress — resilience and self-leadership.
  • Moving forward when feeling stagnant or overloaded — practical and emotional strategies.
  • Communication skills — clarity, conflict navigation, and grounded expression.
  • Time management — healthier structures for demanding environments.
How It Works
