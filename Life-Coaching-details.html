01 — Life Coaching

Come home to who you are.

A space to stop pretending you are fine, and finally look at what is happening inside you — so you can rebuild from a place of clarity, strength and truth.

The Work

For the moments life feels stuck.

Life Coaching is for the moments when life feels stuck, when your boundaries have slipped, and when you quietly realise you are not who you thought you would be by now — or who you are.

It is honest work. Together we slow everything down so you can hear yourself again, untangle the noise, and rebuild from a place of clarity, strength and truth.

75-Minute 1:1 Session

Some focus areas include

  • Breathwork and guided meditation — grounding first and finding home.
  • Emotional regulation — building tools for steadiness.
  • Organising the thought process — decluttering the mind, structuring thoughts, reducing overwhelm.
  • Communication skills — expressing needs, boundaries, and truth without fear.
  • Positive and constructive thinking — shifting from spiralling thoughts to focus and clarity.
  • Time management — creating realistic rhythms that support emotional and mental wellbeing.
