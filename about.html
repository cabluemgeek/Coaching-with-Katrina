How I Coach

A few things I believe.

Genuine, in private and public

A person's character shows most in how they act when nothing's at stake. Coaching should hold you to that same standard.

Boundaries protect the work

Healthy boundaries aren't walls — they're what keep others from overstepping into the life you're trying to build.

Regulate before you respond

Without emotional regulation, every disagreement becomes an outburst instead of a conversation. We build that skill first.

Suggestions, not scripts

I ask thought-provoking questions, and offer a suggestion when you're stuck, never a one-size-fits-all answer.
