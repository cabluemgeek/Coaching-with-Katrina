Hi, I'm Katrina.

My Story

Katrina is a leadership coach, entrepreneur, former university teacher and English educator who spent ten years teaching English, including eight years guiding first, second and third year degree students at university.

Her passion for understanding people led her beyond the classroom and into coaching. Through her own journey of overcoming childhood criticism, grief, failure, panic attacks, self-doubt nad life's many challenges, together with years of coaching people from diverse cultures and backgrounds, she has developed a compassionate approach that helps people understand themselves before trying to change themselves.

Katrina believes that true leadership does not begin by leading others.

It begins by leading yourself.

Her work is not about changing who people are.

It is about helping them remember who they have always been.

Because when we remember who we truly are, we do not simply change our lives. We begin leading them.

Your journey with Katrina

Deep probing questions

The kind that make you pause, breathe, and give yourself permission to be honest with yourself.

Thought-provoking reflections

That gently open the door to perspectives you may not have considered before, helping you see yourself, your choices and your circumstances with greater clarity.

Space to quiet the noise

And untangle the thoughts, fears, expectations and doubts that can make it difficult to hear your own voice.

Tools for self discovery

That help you understand yourself more deeply and continue exploring your thoughts, patterns and possibilities long after our sessions.

Guidance for self-healing

That helps you understand the experiences that have shaped you, without allowing them to define who you are or where you are going.

Support in moving beyong self sabotaging patterns

By recognising the habits and thoughts patterns that may be keeping you in familiar places, even when part of you is ready for something more.

Practical CBT based tools

To help you understand and reshape the thoughts that may be holding you back, giving you practical ways to approach challenges with greater awareness and confidence.

Breathwork and guided meditation

To help you slow down, reconnect with yourself and create moments of calm and presence. These practices can offer a gentle pause from the noise of everyday life, allowing you settle, reflect and become more aware of what you are feeling and return home which is your peaceful state.
