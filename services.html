01 - Life

Life Coaching

Life Coaching is for the moments when life feels stuck, when your boundaries have slipped, and when you quietly realise you are not who you thought you would be by now or who you are.

It is honest work. It is a space to stop pretending you are fine and finally look at what is happening inside you. Together we slow everything down so you can hear yourself again, untangle the noise and rebuild from a place of clarity, strength and truth.

75 minute 1:1 session - some focus areas include:

  • Breathwork and guided meditation - grounding first and finding home.
  • Emotional regulation - building tools for steadiness.
  • Organising the thought process - decluttering the mind, structuring thoughts, reducing overwhelm.
  • Communication skills - expressing needs, boundaries, and truth without feeling afraid or nurvous.
  • Positive and constructive thinking - shifting from spiralling thoughts to focus and clarity.
  • Time management - creating realistic rythms that support emotional and mental well being.

02 - Corporate

1:1 Corporate Coaching

Corporate coaching is a long-term wellbeing partnership designed for companies who want healthier, more resilient teams. This is offered as structured packages: 3-month, 6-month, 12-month, and extended multi-year plans.

Focus areas include :

  • Burnout prevention and cure - stabilising, regulating, and rebuilding capacity.
  • Emotinal regulation - tools employees can use in real time.
  • Handling extreme workplace stress - focusing on resilience and self-leadership.
  • Moving foreward when feeling stagnant or overloaded - practival and emotional strategies.
  • Communication skills - clarity, conflict navigation, and grounded expression.
  • Time management - healthier structres for demanding environments.

Terms to be discussed and agreed upon in person.
